#### Adepta Sororitas: Kill Team Sanctifiers Kit Build Options

Because the Sanctifiers Kit is a Kill Team kit, it has a heavy set of alternate builds, and a lot of different model types. I'm going to try and list that all out here. First, I'm going to list out what the unit model options are.

_Unit Model Options_

- 1x Miraculist
- 1x Salvationist
- 1x Death Cult Assassin
- 1x Missionary with Flamer
- 1x Missionary with Meltagun
- Sanctifier with Hand Flamer (looks like you can take 2-4 of these?)
- 1x Sanctifier with two Hand Flamers (looks like if you take 1 of these, you take 1 less Sanctifier with Hand Flamer)
- 1x Sanctifier with Simulacrum Imperialis (looks like if you take 1 of these, you take 1 less Sanctifier with Hand Flamer)

_Kit Model Options_

The kit adds 2 additional models that appear to be relevant to Kill Team only. From the sources for 40k, from both BSData and Wahapedia, the 40k unit is only the 9 models above. The kit itself contains 11 models, including the following two models:

- Confessor
- Cherub

As a side note, the following are the names of some of the models above variants:

- Sanctifier with two hand flamers = Sanctifier Conflagrator
- Sanctifier with Simulacrum Imperialis = Sanctifier Reliquant
